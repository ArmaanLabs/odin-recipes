Shahi Panner

Description

Fit for royalty, Shahi Paneer is one of North India’s most celebrated vegetarian delicacies. Soft, pillowy cubes of cottage cheese (paneer) are gently simmered in an ultra-luxurious, satiny gravy built from ground cashews, tomatoes, aromatics, and rich cream. Infused with delicate whole spices, a pinch of cardamom, and subtle undertones of saffron, it delivers an indulgent, mildly sweet, and fragrant dish with virtually no harsh heat. Elegant enough for festive occasions yet simple to bring together at home, it pairs naturally with warm tandoori roti, garlic naan, or fragrant jeera rice.

Ingredients

  • Paneer: 300–400 g (cubed)
  • Onions: 2 medium (chopped)
  • Tomatoes: 3 medium (chopped)
  • Cashews: 12–15 pieces (for the creamy gravy)
  • Ginger-garlic paste: 1 tbsp
  • Butter or Ghee: 2 tbsp
  • Spices: 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili, ½ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp garam masala, salt to taste
  • Fresh cream: 2–3 tbsp
  • Sugar or Honey: ½ tsp (to balance tanginess)
  • Kasuri methi: 1 tsp (crushed, optional finish)

Steps :

  1. Boil the base: In a pan, combine the chopped onions, tomatoes, cashews, ginger-garlic paste, and ½ cup water. Cover and simmer over medium heat for 10 to 12 minutes until soft, then let it cool.
  2. Blend smooth: Transfer the cooled mixture to a blender and grind until completely smooth and creamy.
  3. Bloom the spices: Melt the butter or ghee in a pan over low heat. Add the Kashmiri red chili powder, turmeric, and salt, stirring for 20 to 30 seconds so the spices release their aroma without burning.
  4. Cook the gravy: Pour the blended puree into the pan. Stir well and simmer on medium-low heat for 5 to 6 minutes until the gravy thickens, then mix in the sugar or honey.
  5. Finish with paneer and cream: Add the cubed paneer, garam masala, and fresh cream. Simmer gently on low heat for 3 to 4 minutes, stir in the crushed kasuri methi, and take it off the flame.
