Fried Chicken

Description

Ultra-crispy and golden on the outside, succulent and tender on the inside, this classic Fried Chicken delivers the ultimate crunch with every bite. Soaked in a seasoned buttermilk brine to lock in moisture, each piece is dredged in a seasoned flour crust packed with garlic, onion, paprika, and cracked black pepper before frying to deep-golden perfection. Reliable, deeply satisfying, and simple to pull off at home, it is a guaranteed crowd-pleaser for casual family dinners, game days, and weekend comfort feasts.

Ingredients

  • Chicken: 750 g (bone-in pieces or tenders)
  • Buttermilk (or milk + 1 tbsp vinegar): 1 cup (tenderizes the meat)
  • All-purpose flour (maida): 1.5 cups
  • Cornstarch (cornflour): ¼ cup (key for extra crunch)
  • Egg: 1 large
  • Seasoning mix: 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp paprika, ½ tsp black pepper, salt to taste
  • Cooking oil: for deep-frying

Steps :

  1. Marinate the chicken: In a large bowl, whisk together the buttermilk, egg, and half of the seasoning mix (garlic powder, paprika, black pepper, and salt). Submerge the chicken pieces in the mixture, cover, and let soak for at least 30 minutes (or up to 2 hours in the fridge).
  2. Prepare the flour dredge: In a shallow dish, whisk together the all-purpose flour, cornstarch, and the remaining half of the seasoning mix until evenly combined.
  3. Dredge and coat: Lift each piece of chicken from the buttermilk marinade, letting excess liquid drip off, then press it firmly into the flour mixture on all sides to coat completely. (For extra crunchy craggy bits, drizzle 1–2 tablespoons of the leftover buttermilk marinade into the flour and pinch it together before coating).
  4. Heat the oil: Pour cooking oil into a deep pan or Dutch oven until it is at least 2 inches deep. Heat over medium heat to around 175°C (350°F)—a drop of flour should sizzle immediately without burning.
  5. Fry in batches: Carefully lower the chicken pieces into the hot oil, cooking 3 to 4 pieces at a time so the pan is not overcrowded. Fry bone-in pieces for 12 to 14 minutes (turning occasionally) or tenders for 6 to 8 minutes, until the crust is deep golden brown and the meat is cooked through.
  6. Drain and rest: Transfer the fried chicken to a wire rack or paper towel-lined plate to drain excess oil. Let it rest for 3 to 5 minutes before serving so the crust stays shatteringly crisp.
