Butter Chicken

Description

Loved worldwide for good reason, Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani) strikes the ultimate balance between rich comfort and aromatic flavor. Tender, yogurt-marinated chicken is seared until lightly charred, then simmered in a silky, mildly spiced gravy made from ripe tomatoes, cream, and real butter. It is smooth, aromatic, and comforting without overpowering heat—making it an instant hit for family dinners, curry newcomers, and seasoned home cooks alike. Best served warm with garlic naan or steamed basmati rice

Ingredients

  1. Chicken & Marinade
    • Boneless chicken thighs: 750 g (cut into bite-sized 1.5-inch pieces; thighs stay far juicier than breast)
    • Thick plain yogurt / hung curd: ½ cup (120 g)
    • Ginger-garlic paste: 2 tbsp
    • Kashmiri red chili powder: 1.5 tbsp (provides vibrant red color with mild, gentle heat)
    • Garam masala: 1 tsp
    • Ground cumin: 1 tsp
    • Lemon juice: 1 tbsp
    • Mustard oil or vegetable oil: 1 tbsp
    • Salt: 1 tsp (or to taste)
  2. Makhani Gravy (Sauce Base)
    • Butter: 3 tbsp (divided: 1 tbsp to start, 2 tbsp to finish)
    • Neutral oil: 1 tbsp (prevents butter from burning)
    • Fresh ripe tomatoes: 500–600 g (roughly chopped; choose red, ripe plum or Roma tomatoes)
    • Onion: 1 medium (roughly chopped; keep onions light so tomatoes shine)
    • Raw cashews: 15–20 pieces (soaked in warm water for 15 mins; creates the velvety body)
    • Ginger-garlic paste: 1 tbsp
    • Whole green cardamoms: 4 pods
    • Cloves: 3–4 pieces
    • Cinnamon stick: 1 inch
    • Water: ¾ cup (to simmer tomato-cashew base)
  3. Seasoning & Finishing
    • Kashmiri red chili powder: 1 tsp (for the gravy color)
    • Garam masala: ½ tsp
    • Sugar or honey: 1 to 1.5 tsp (balances the acidity of the tomatoes)
    • Heavy cream / fresh cream: ¼ cup (60 ml)
    • Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves): 1 tbsp (lightly toasted on a dry pan and crushed between palms)
    • Salt: to taste

Steps :

1. Marinate the chicken
In a bowl, mix the boneless chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, Kashmiri red chili powder, garam masala, ground cumin, lemon juice, oil, and salt. Cover and let it marinate for at least 30 minutes (or overnight in the fridge).
2. Sear the chicken
Heat 1 tablespoon of oil or butter in a heavy pan or skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken pieces in a single layer for 3 to 4 minutes per side until lightly charred and mostly cooked through. Set aside on a plate.
3. Simmer the gravy base
In the same pan, melt 1 tablespoon of butter. Add the chopped tomatoes, onions, cashews, ginger-garlic paste, whole spices (cardamom, cloves, cinnamon), and ½ cup of water. Cover and simmer over medium heat for 12 to 15 minutes until tomatoes and onions are soft and mushy.
4. Blend to a silky puree
Turn off the heat and discard the cinnamon stick. Let the mixture cool slightly, then transfer to a blender and process until completely smooth. Pass the pureed sauce through a fine-mesh strainer directly into the pan for that signature velvety restaurant texture.
5. Bring the sauce together
Place the strained gravy back over low-medium heat. Stir in Kashmiri red chili powder, garam masala, salt to taste, and 1 teaspoon of sugar or honey to balance the acidity. Simmer for 3 to 5 minutes until it thickens slightly.
6. Combine and finish
Stir in the seared chicken pieces along with any resting juices. Add 2 tablespoons of butter and ¼ cup of fresh cream, simmering gently for 4 to 5 minutes on low heat so the chicken absorbs the sauce. Finish by crushing toasted kasuri methi between your palms directly into the curry. Turn off the heat and serve warm with naan or rice.
